License & data sources

Everything written for this course (slides, labs, explainers, code) is free to reuse. The data it runs on is a mix: some collected for this course, most borrowed from published work. This page says which is which, and how to cite each one.

COURSE MATERIALS

Slides, labs, explainers, the playground tools and the code that builds them are released under CC BY 4.0: you may reuse and adapt them as long as you credit the source. The third-party datasets and lexicons below are not covered and keep their own terms.

CITE LIKE THIS

Masur, P. K. (2026). Computational analysis of digital communication [Course materials]. Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam. https://masurp.github.io/cadc/

DATASETS USED IN THE COURSE

Dataset What it is Source License / terms Used in
European Social Survey, round 10 37,611 respondents in 22 countries, 2020–22; eight variables on news use, internet use, political interest and trust, missing-value codes kept ESS ERIC, ESS Round 10 integrated file, europeansocialsurvey.org Free for non-commercial use with citation. ESS ERIC asks that its data not be hosted on other websites, so this subset is shared with enrolled students on Canvas only, not on this site Week 1
Gun polls 57 US polls on gun policy after the Parkland shooting, Feb–Mar 2018 FiveThirtyEight, poll-quiz-guns CC BY 4.0 Week 1
New Year’s resolution tweets 5,002 US tweets with #NewYearsResolution, Dec 2014–Jan 2015, each coded into a resolution category CrowdFlower, Data for Everyone library (no longer online) No licence could be confirmed from the publisher, and the texts are tweets (Twitter/X terms). Shared with enrolled students on Canvas only, for teaching; user names removed and @-mentions blanked Weeks 1 and 3
State of the Union speeches 240 annual addresses by US presidents, 1790–2020 The sotu R package (Arnold, 2019), from the American Presidency Project Public domain texts; package GPL-2 Weeks 1 and 2
Corona tweets 2,855 English tweets tagged #corona, 5–14 Nov 2021 Collected by the course team via the Twitter/X API, 2021 edition Tweets (Twitter/X terms). Shared with enrolled students on Canvas only, inside the week 2 lab folders, for teaching; user names removed and @-mentions blanked Weeks 2 and 4
Yelp sentences 1,000 single-sentence restaurant reviews, human-labelled positive/negative Kotzias, Denil, De Freitas & Smyth (2015), “From Group to Individual Labels using Deep Features,” KDD 2015, part of the UCI Sentiment Labelled Sentences set Public research dataset; free to reuse with citation Week 2
NRC Word-Emotion Association Lexicon Word-level emotion and sentiment associations Mohammad & Turney (2013), National Research Council Canada Free for research and teaching; commercial use requires a licence from NRC Week 2
Science abstracts 5,250 arXiv titles and abstracts across three disciplines arXiv.org, sampled via the CSS book’s science.csv teaching corpus arXiv abstracts are freely reusable for research and teaching; original papers keep their own copyright Week 3
GloVe word vectors Pretrained word embeddings (50- and 300-dimensional) Pennington, Socher & Manning (2014), Stanford NLP Group, nlp.stanford.edu/projects/glove Public Domain Dedication and License (PDDL) v1.0 Week 3
Ecosent headlines 6,322 Dutch economic-news headlines with human sentiment codes, incl. the paper’s 284-headline gold standard van Atteveldt, van der Velden & Boukes (2021), github.com/vanatteveldt/ecosent MIT License Weeks 3 and 4
Prompt-lab gold coding 48 film reviews, hand-coded by the course team on three factual fields (also as data/film-reviews.csv with the two derived codebooks) Created for this course CC BY 4.0, same as other course materials Week 4, Playground
Model outputs (results/llm*) Zero-shot and few-shot predictions from open models run locally (Llama, Gemma, Qwen via Ollama) and from Claude (Anthropic API) Generated by the course team for teaching comparisons CC BY 4.0 Week 4

Embeddings are computed with nomic-embed-text, run locally through Ollama. The model is licensed under Apache 2.0, and the six example sentences it embeds in the week 3 deck were written for this course.
