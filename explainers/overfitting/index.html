Week 3 · overfitting

How much freedom is too much

Fifteen points scattered around a curve. Give the model more freedom and it follows the training points ever more closely — including the noise. The question is never "does it fit?" but "does it fit anything that will still be there next time?"

The model

1 = a straight line. 3 = the shape the data really has. 9+ = free to chase every point.

The data
