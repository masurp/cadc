Precision
—
Precision Of the texts your dictionary called positive, how many really were. Falls when your words fire on the wrong texts. hits / (hits + false alarms)
Recall
—
Recall Of the texts a human called positive, how many your dictionary caught. Falls when your words never fire. hits / (hits + misses + no calls)
F1
—
F1 The harmonic mean of the two. One number, and it punishes a big gap between precision and recall. 2 × P × R / (P + R)
Accuracy
—
Accuracy Verdicts that matched the human code, over all 20 texts. A text your dictionary said nothing about counts as wrong. (hits + correct) / all texts
Coverage
—
Coverage Texts where at least one of your words fired. Not a performance score — a check on whether the instrument spoke at all. (all texts − silent) / all texts
