Part I · Four generations of method
Weeks 1 to 4

One lecture, two labs and one homework assignment a week. Every week adds a generation of method. From week 2 on, each one is scored against texts coded by hand, including the gold standard you code yourself in week 2.

Part II · Exam
Week 5

The unaided lane: everything from the first four weeks, in the room, on your own, with no model to ask.

Week 05

Exam

No lecture and no labs this week: it is yours to prepare. The exam itself is not a coding test. You read output, you read code, and you argue about whether a measurement can be believed.

  • On TestVision · multiple choice and open questions
  • Ends 18:15 if you have an approved extra-time arrangement
  • Interpreting R output, reading code, critiquing a design
  • You are never asked to write code from scratch
  • Questions built on the data, code and traps from the labs
  • 40% of the final grade
